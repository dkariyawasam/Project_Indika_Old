## Lt Surge
- Trainer Class: Officer

Surge is a veteran soldier from the Pokemon war, he holds the title of Lieutenant and comes from Unova.

- On first entering the gym, Surge is unavailable.
- The gym aide heads over to you and tells you Surge is at the Diglett Cave training.
- Go to Diglett Cave, find Surge and he tells you this is the only place he can go full out when training, he leaves to go back to the gym
- At the gym, if you talk to the assistant, he tells you about Electric type weaknesses
- Challenge Surge and defeat him
- After defeating Surge, he tells you he has a pokemon that has evolved through trading while holding an item but will only give it to a strong trainer, he is willing to trade you one if you show him you can train a pokemon fully, he gives you a Pichu, once fully evolved he will trade you it.
- Discounts for EV items can be unlocked with the Thunder Badge.
