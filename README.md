# Pokemon Expeditions: Kanto

Experience an all-new Kanto as a field researcher for Professor Oak as you uncover the mysteries of evolution and the bonds between Pokemon and trainers.

## Features

- Scalable Gym leaders: Gym leaders 1-7 scale in difficulty depending on the order you challenge them in.
- Openworld: There are no roadblocks
- The wild encounter pool is from classic Kanto + HGSS
- Trade/Trade with item/Friendship evolution Pokemon cannot be found in the wild but will be given out by Gym Leaders and special trainers.
- Alpha Pokemon: Move learn evolution Pokemon cannot be found in the wild but will appear as unique overworld encounters
